## Abstract

A longitudinal, empirical analysis of the FCC's 13-year Measuring Broadband America (MBA) dataset, comprising over 3.5 billion latency observations. Critiques current measurement methodology and discusses implications for the FCC's proposed Broadband Consumer Label.
