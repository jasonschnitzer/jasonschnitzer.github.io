## Abstract

Compares Low Latency DOCSIS (LLD) and Low Latency xHaul (LLX) approaches for mobile backhaul over DOCSIS networks, and proposes a "Latency Mitigation Function" using programmable MAC-layer control alongside DOCSIS QoS mechanisms (PGS, RTPS, UGS, PCMM).
