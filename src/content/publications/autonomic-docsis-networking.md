## Abstract

Demonstrates self-optimization and self-configuration of cable access networks via closed-loop (MAPE: Monitor–Analyze–Plan–Execute) control, applied to two problems: self-optimizing DOCSIS 4.0 OFDM downstream channel modulation profiles (subcarrier bit-loading at the PHY layer), and self-configuring MAC-layer QoS resources for low-latency mobile backhaul.
