I'm a Senior Research Associate in the Department of Computer Science at the University of Colorado Boulder, where I lead the Broadband Measurement Clearinghouse (BCLEAR).

My work sits at the intersection of network management, broadband measurement, and broadband policy — largely grounded in DOCSIS/HFC access networks, with an emerging interest in ultra-low-latency links and reinforcement-learning agents for autonomic network control more broadly. My Ph.D. work developed closed-loop (MAPE: Monitor–Analyze–Plan–Execute) autonomic control for DOCSIS 4.0 access networks. More recently, I've been working on how broadband availability and reliability get measured and defined for policy, including the FCC's Measuring Broadband America program and the BEAD program's network availability requirements.

## Research Interests

- Autonomic and programmable DOCSIS networking (closed-loop MAPE control)
- DOCSIS/HFC protocol and network management
- Low-latency access networking and 5G/6G convergence
- Broadband performance measurement
- Broadband and telecom policy
