## Talk Summary

A 20-minute presentation, co-authored with D. Reed, on the paper of the same name. Covers why BEAD's 99.45% availability requirement is measurement-methodology-agnostic, how that creates wildly different real-world reliability outcomes at the same headline number, and a proposed path to a standards-grounded definition using ITU-T Y.1540's PIA framework paired with an RFC 9544-style Mean Time Between Violations metric.

See the corresponding [publication](/publications/broadband-after-bead) for the full paper.
